It was the study of radioactivity that forced open a new window. Here, for the first time, individual electrons could be traced, emerging from radium with velocities that crept up towards that of light itself. With such swiftness the old theories began to stammer. What this compels us to do is to redraw the frontier between what belongs to the observer and what belongs to the event observed.
